## Core watch areas

### 🇪🇺 EU AI Act / European AI Office
Implementation, enforcement, transparency, risk obligations and official European guidance.

**Official framework:** https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai

### 🌐 WAICO
World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization: multilateral AI cooperation, institutional development and membership watch.

**Official founding reference:** https://www.mfa.gov.cn/eng/wjbzhd/202607/t20260717_11984747.html

### 🧭 NIST
AI Risk Management Framework, testing, measurement, safety and technical standards.

**Official:** https://www.nist.gov/artificial-intelligence

### 🏛 OECD.AI · UNESCO · Council of Europe
Policy observatory, AI principles, ethics, rights and international frameworks.

- OECD.AI: https://oecd.ai/
- UNESCO AI ethics: https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics
- Council of Europe AI Convention: https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence/the-framework-convention-on-artificial-intelligence
