## Current AI intelligence

The generated dataset is refreshed automatically from curated AI sources and provider monitors. It contains source headlines, brief excerpts, canonical links, categories, provider tags and timestamps.

**[→ Open the current live dataset](../data/news.json)**

### Signal categories

`Frontier Models` · `Products & Agents` · `Research` · `Infrastructure` · `Open Source` · `Industry` · `Safety & Security` · `Compliance & Ethics`

### What is tracked

- 42 curated AI sources across labs, research, journalism and governance
- 18 LLM / foundation-model provider monitors
- first-party announcements plus independent reporting
- AI-only filtering and deduplication
- provider matching and provenance
- scheduled refresh every 30 minutes

### Source-first policy

Full articles are not mirrored. Each item keeps its canonical publisher URL. Only headlines and brief source excerpts are included in the generated feed.
