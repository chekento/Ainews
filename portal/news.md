## Current AI intelligence

The generated dataset is refreshed automatically from curated AI sources and provider monitors. It contains source headlines, brief excerpts, canonical links, categories, provider tags and timestamps.

**[→ Open the current live dataset](../data/news.json)**

### Signal categories

`Frontier Models` · `Products & Agents` · `Research` · `Infrastructure` · `Robotics & Embodied AI` · `Open Source` · `Industry` · `Safety & Security` · `Compliance & Ethics`

### What is tracked

- **60 curated AI sources** across labs, research, journalism, infrastructure, safety and governance
- **18 LLM / foundation-model provider monitors**
- first-party announcements plus independent reporting
- strict AI-only filtering and deduplication
- provider matching, provenance and signal metadata
- scheduled refresh every 30 minutes

### Source-first policy

Full articles are not mirrored. Each item keeps its canonical publisher URL. Only headlines, brief feed/monitor excerpts and metadata are used in the generated feed. Publisher artwork is used only when explicitly supplied by the feed.

### Beta data-quality note

Automated relevance, provider matching, categorization, clustering and summaries are still under development. A high signal score or AI-relevance label is not a guarantee of factual accuracy. Use the original publisher as the authoritative destination.
